# Qt-Greenhouse-interface

We have three greenhouses growing tomatoes and peppers. We have installed sensors and actuators to control irrigation, roof vents, heating, and shading screens. However, the controller has a text-based interface that only the installer understands.
We want an application for the touchscreen panel in the irrigation control room and for the office computer.
Last winter, we lost part of our crop due to an overnight frost that nobody noticed in time.
  -Simulator data: indoor and outdoor temperature and humidity, soil moisture levels by sector, solar radiation, and the status of pumps, valves,     windows, heating systems, and shading screens.

  - Faults that can be simulated: frost, pump failure, sensor data loss, and water supply interruption.
  - Available users to consult: farmers, agricultural cooperative technicians, teaching staff, and students from the Higher Technical School of       Agricultural Engineering and the Natural Environment (ETSIAMN) at the UPV.
